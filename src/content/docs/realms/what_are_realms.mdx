title: What are Realms?
description: An explanation of what Realms are, how they work and what problem they solve
---
Realms are the flagship feature in Lorealm. Everything builds on top of it.

The simplest way to think about a Realm is like a stage play. An empty Realm is an
empty stage. Not very exciting. However, you can add things to that stage, such as an
actor. Maybe a prop. Maybe you need a lighting system and a sound system...

Realms work the exact same way.

## Why are they useful
### Distribution
Realms are designed to be distributable. It builds on top of the *"Weaver Template
Notation"* (WTN) to let very complex setups be distributed through a single
text file. If you're familiar with how character sharing works, then this is the exact
same thing. But instead of sharing a single character, you're sharing an entire system.

A Realm can be shared with a collection of attached resources, or with an "input schema"
which lets the receiver use resources from their own setup using the imported realm.
That means you can create a Realm that lets you express a strategy game, for example.
Then when you share that Realm with someone, they can use their own characters as actors
in that Realm.

Lorealm already ships with a Realm like this, in fact! A simple group chat system! You
can open it right now and have a look at it if you'd like!

### Expressiveness
With a Realm, you can express your creativity like never before! There are virtually
no limits to what you can achieve with the Realm system. Here's a few examples of what
you could make:
- A strategy game where you play against LLM-controlled leaders
- A full world where each agent can only perceive what's in sight
- A murder mystery where each suspect knows only their own alibi and secrets, and you solve it by questioning them
- A tabletop-style campaign with an LLM game master and a script agent that enforces the rules: dice, HP and inventory
- A survival scenario where a script agent runs weather, hunger and day/night, and the characters react to it
- A story where you aren't the main character, just one more person in a world that carries on without you
- And so, so much more...
